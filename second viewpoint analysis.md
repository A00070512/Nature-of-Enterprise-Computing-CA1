## 4.1 Introduction to societal viewpoint:

This is the sociological perspective which considers the effect that technology can have on people and society. Technological innovations have significantly affected our society and the way in which we interact, earn a living, and educate ourselves. Throughout history, new technologies such as personal computers, the internet, and modern operating systems have been developed so that technology can be both more widely available and beneficial. Nevertheless, alongside these advances there are issues relating to confidentiality, social isolation and technology addiction.
