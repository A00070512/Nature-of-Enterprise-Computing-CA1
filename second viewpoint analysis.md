## 4. Second viewpoint: Societal

### 4.1 Introduction to societal viewpoint:

This is the sociological perspective which considers the effect that technology can have on people and society. Technological innovations have significantly affected our society and the way in which we interact, earn a living, and educate ourselves. Throughout history, new technologies such as personal computers, the internet, and modern operating systems have been developed so that technology can be both more widely available and beneficial. Nevertheless, alongside these advances there are issues relating to confidentiality, social isolation and technology addiction. This essay will analyse the impacts of six key areas of computer technology.

### 4.2 Impact of personal computers on society:

Whilst previously computers were mainly utilised by large companies and institutions, recent innovations have enabled computers to be adopted by members of the general public. Firms including IBM, Apple and Microsoft have all played their part in promoting computer usage. Eventually, computers were widely adopted within both the home, educational and work environments. As well as furthering peoples access to learning and information. Computers have enabled many day to day activities to be conducted in a more time effective manner. Unfortunately, not everybody has the ability to purchase computers and so there is now a "digital divide" which separates individuals who can and cannot use technology.

### 4.3 Impact of the internet on communication:

The internet was developed from ARPANET and then became the World Wide Web. This has allowed individuals to easily share and communicate information with others in various countries. Furthermore, the internet has enhanced peoples ability to gain an education and conduct research, also enabling fast access to information. Alongside these benefits, there are issues which have risen including false information, cyberbullying and data security concerns.

### 4.4 Programming languages and accessibility: 

Development in programming have moved on from using Assembly code and now include more up to date languages such as Python. Compared to previous languages, Python has a much clearer syntax which can be more easily understood by beginners. Therefore, more individuals have been able to educate themselves in programming. Moreover, it has led to employment opportunities computing and further technology areas. 

### 4.5 Operating systems in everyday life:

Systems have been developed which have enhanced the usability of computers. Systems including Unix and Linux have helped to further modern technology. Furthermore, operating system such as Windows, macOS, android and iOS have enabled more people to engage with technological equipment. Technology is therefore now easier to use. Alongside this, society is becoming increasingly reliant on technology in areas such as communication, learning, and employment.

### 4.6 Database development and personal information:

Database technology has enhanced an organisations ability to store and control information. Hospitals, banks, educational and business establishments are now able to effectively handle vast quantities of information. This has led to improved access of services and enhanced ease of information retrieval. Despite these benefits, there are growing concerns regarding peoples privacy and the security of personal information together the potential for information to be wrongly used.

### 4.7 Evolution of human-computer interaction:

Advances in computer applications have seen a shift from text, to a graphical user interface (GUI) and then further on to virtual and augmented reality (VR/AR). GUI's include many visual elements such as icons, windows and menus, therefore allowing the general public to utilise computers. Furthermore, VR and AR has brought about various innovative applications within areas including gaming, leisure and education. Unfortunately, more sophisticated technology can often be costly so may not be available for some.

### 4.8 Overall influence of computing in society:

Overall, computing has led to a number of changes within society which have improved aspects such as communication, learning, and working. Therefore, technology has become highly embedded in our lives and has provided new opportunities. However, there are now problems relating to digital divide, data protection and also reliance on technology. Thus, whilst computing has evidently provided many positive impacts upon society, it can also cause problems which should be analysed and dealt with.
